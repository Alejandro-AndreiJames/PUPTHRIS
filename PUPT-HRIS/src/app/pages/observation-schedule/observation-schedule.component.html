Areas for Observation

No. Criteria
1 Instruction and discussion facilitation referring to sharing control and direction with students.
2 Commitment referring to the course specialist's act or quality of fulfilling responsibility giving the dedication, discipline, and maturity for the learner's development and advancement.
3 Teaching for independent learning pertaining to the course specialist's ability to organize the teaching-learning process to enable learners maximize their potential.
4 Use instructional materials and other educational resources to help maximize learning.
5 Classroom climate and virtual community referring to facilitating collaborative action enabling learners to communicate openly and effectively.
6 Course organization referring to objectives, concepts, examples, and program fragments discussed in class.
7 Assessments referring to the activities required in the course to assess the competence of the students.

Note: These are the criteria that will be used during your classroom observation. Please ensure your preparation addresses these areas for the best evaluation outcome.
